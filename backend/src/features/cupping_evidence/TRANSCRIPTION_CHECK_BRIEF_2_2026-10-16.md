Their output was diffed against brief 2 Parts B, C, D. Every material discrepancy below was then re-checked on the image by the review session; those are marked **verified**. Items marked *blind reader* were not re-checked.

**Result in one line.** Of roughly 150 numeric cells, about 12 differ, all in the same direction: a mark that is crossed out or scribbled over on the paper was counted as a live mark in brief 2. Words, calls and family ticks match except for five struck words loaded as said and one dropped call note. Nothing was invented; the errors are all "strike not seen".

3. `cupping_sessions.transcribed_by / transcribed_at / checked_by` (review item C4) are filled for these five sessions with the blind check named.

## Decisions (Dana, 2026-10-24). Do not re-open.

1. **Box order by adjacency.** For a matched customer: her archetype first, then the archetypes adjacent to it in `v_coffee_archetype_adjacency` (hop-derived, the same data behind the "Worth exploring" badge) by `hop_count` desc then `avg_confidence` desc, then the remaining archetypes in `coffee_archetype.sort_order`. Unmatched visitor: `sort_order`, as today. Ties are broken by `sort_order`. No distance, no vector. The adjacency view is symmetric (`archetype_a` / `archetype_b`), so read both columns.
2. **Slot suggestion from bands only.** `getDialSuggestion` suggests a slot only when every active slot of the archetype has `spec_band_lo/hi`; otherwise it returns `null` with `reason: 'no_band'` and the list of slots missing a band. The dial signal is still recorded on every merge and `refreshDialSignal` (the pooled midpoint is evidence); only `suggested_slot` is null, and the signal's `notes` says `no suggestion: slot(s) <n, n> of <archetype> have no band`. `BASIS_ARCHETYPE_VECTOR` and the `v_archetype_dimension_comparison` query go. The workbench (Dimensions tab, suggestion column) shows "no band on slot N" in grey instead of a suggestion; the Coffees-page evidence panel the same. `v_archetype_dimension_comparison` stays as the admin's hypothesis-vs-actual view.
3. **After 1 and 2, `archetype_vector` has no reader outside admin.** `comments.sql` Purpose: "Seed hypothesis (May 2026 assumed targets). Read only by v_archetype_vectors and v_archetype_dimension_comparison (admin). No customer-facing or decision-making reader since coffee presentation brief 3." `lint:lineage` readers list updated. The table and seed stay.
4. **The story follows the description's rules.** Dashes replaced after generation as brief 1 does for the description; flavor words come from the roaster voice (`getRoasterVoice`: signature then accent, not the old `topDescriptors`) and are attributed ("the roaster calls it"), with "and we tasted it too" where confirmed; no "our cupping" as our own tasting; no archetype name as a category; the leak guard (roaster name, raw coffee name, award tokens) as brief 1 D4 already applies. **Region-level origin stays**: the story page says where the coffee is from at region level by design (HOME_TASK_5, "the highlands of X"), and that is not a leak. The story's own specificity reject-and-retry (farm, co-op, lot, importer) is unchanged.
5. **Regenerate stories** for active coffees where `story_admin_edited = false` after the prompt change (force), none where it is true. Print 2914 and 2917.

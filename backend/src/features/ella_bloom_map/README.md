Two findings from the 2026-11-10 code survey, for Dana:

- **Slots are not only the dial's.** `subscriptionService` (line ~166) and `subscriptionShipment` resolve what to ship by `coffee_dial_slot` (archetype × sort order, landing default); `householdReads`, `coffeeCardReads`, `sommelierRag`, `blendResolver` read slots or `v_coffee_sellable_slot`. So in this workstream "slot ladder retired" means: the map, the match and the hops never read a slot, and the price readers the map needs go to `coffee_retail_price`. Subscriptions and shipments keep slots until a commerce brief moves them to coffee grain; that brief is not in this plan and needs its own decision.
- `dial_archetype_config` was dropped in Catalog Blueprint 5a; `coffee_archetype` owns `has_bloom_dial`, `is_archetype`, `dominant_dimension_id`. The map's ruler per wedge is a different fact from the quiz's dominant dimension (Floral: dominant 9, ruler acidity), so it lives on the new `dial_wedge` row, not on `coffee_archetype`.

## Decisions 2026-11-12 (Dana, after taking the quiz against brief 3's `/map`)

1. **The customer sees one Bloom Dial: the current visual, fed by the new logic.** Camila's Bloom Dial stays the picture on the quiz result and The Bloom page, drawn from `v_dial_placement` (each family's coffees in ring order, the customer's dot on her coffee, tap only). The hub face (`EllaMapFace`) is admin-only, on `/admin/map`. Rev 3 section 7 and brief 3's `MapQuizStart` thumb are superseded on this point.
2. **The customer match page is cards only, no map.** One coffee big, then the alternatives, each with one reason: the map's own move line, relative to the coffee she stands on. The old compatibility sentence and comfort-zone labels (archetype-profile logic, Website 5) do not appear on these cards.
3. **Two states on the match page.** Before any bag: the starting coffee and the steps she could take instead. After a bag: Same again, bolder, gentler, something different. "Same again" never shows before a first bag.
4. **Everything stays hidden until the logic is solid.** Pages are built behind `dial_surface` rows on `lane`; nothing switches until Dana says so.
5. **Order of work:** Usual Drink Rev 4 (the three customer questions, capture only; `backend/src/features/usual_drink/`), then a Match page Rev 2 brief for points 1 to 3, then brief 4 (backfill, prices, switch, retire).

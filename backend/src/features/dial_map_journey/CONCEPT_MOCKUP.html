One graph, two lenses. The Map is the world: every coffee in its slot, every hop, every seam. Click any coffee to stand on it — the Journey lens shows the world from there.
How to read the map

Each row is an archetype's dial: four labeled slots, gentle on the left, bold on the right. Solid pills are coffees in their home slot (★ = default; black square = Path, outlined = Temecula). Dashed pills are guests — coffees whose home is another dial, welding two families at a touching edge. Curved lines are hops, colored by the dimension you travel on; hops between rows are the seams. Empty slots with an amber tint are open range — positions with no coffee yet. Use the dimension chips to see what travels on a single flavor knob.

How to read the journey

You are standing on the coffee in the center. The four flavor dimensions are the compass: each spoke is one move — more one way, less the other. Nearby chips are one hop away; distance shows the size of the jump (Δ). A dashed empty circle at the end of a spoke means no move exists in that direction — a dead end, deliberate or missing. Click any chip to travel there; your trail is recorded above. The horizon rows below show everything reachable in two or three turns.

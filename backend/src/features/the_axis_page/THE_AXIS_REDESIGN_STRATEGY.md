## 1. The strategic shift

| | Current page | New page |
|---|---|---|
| Thesis | "Black Box Transparency" — here is the finished engine | **"Watch the data work"** — here is a living data system you can see capture, structure, connect, learn, and tighten |
| Protagonist | The matching engine | The data and its journey; AI as a named character |
| Proof style | Asserted ("proprietary vector mapping") | Demonstrated (real lineage, real provenance, real aggregate signals) |
| AI stance | Deliberately unnamed | Named and located: Liam reads the map; AI helps build and tune it |
| Posture | Blueprint of a black box | Window into a live system |

Why it's stronger: it shows the work instead of claiming a result; almost no coffee brand can show an actual data pipeline; and it's literally true to how the product is built (the Bloom Dial base-data session is the proof case). It also matches where the market is: transparency now outranks price as the #1 trust driver for shoppers, half of consumers want brands to say clearly when and how they use AI, and "radical transparency" / show-don't-tell is a named 2026 branding trend.

Brand fit is direct: *System Led, Data Driven* and *System Integrity* become the page's subject, not just its backstage. *Structure Is Visible* (Visual Foundations) is almost a brief for this page.

## 2. The competitive-safety rule (governs everything below)

**Show the journey and the evidence. Never the recipe.**

The page must prove the system is real and alive without becoming a free template. Three disclosure tiers:

**Tier A — Show freely (already public or non-replicable without doing the work):**
- The five archetypes and their names (already on site/bags).
- The lifecycle narrative: capture → structure → connect → consume → refine.
- Provenance lineage: WCR Sensory Lexicon 2.0 → SCA CVA Descriptive Assessment → our dimensions. Copying this claim requires actually running calibrated cuppings — it's a moat, not a leak.
- That coffees form a connected, navigable network (the *idea* of the graph).
- That Liam is a named AI sommelier who reads structured data, and that AI helps tune positions as cuppings accrue.

**Tier B — Show as live aggregates only (proves liveness, reveals nothing structural):**
- Counters: coffees mapped, cupping sessions logged, feedback signals received, positions refined this quarter, "map last tightened on {date}".
- An abstracted rendering of the real graph: real node/edge *counts and density*, but unlabeled nodes, no coordinates, no dimension labels on edges.
- Aggregate movement: "12 coffees moved position this quarter based on your feedback" — evidence of the loop without exposing the mechanics.

**Tier C — Never show (the recipe):**
- Spread-for-connectivity placement philosophy; seam/guest-position mechanics; dial slot naming logic.
- Quiz branch logic and its mapping to multi-edge archetype pairs.
- Schema/table/view names, the SQL-RAG architecture, intent-classification and routing internals.
- Exact dimension weights, distance function, computed-position formulas, the 0–15 scale as an operational detail (lineage can be named; internal scoring mechanics cannot).

Litmus test for any future content: *could a competitor's PM screenshot this section and hand it to their engineers as a spec?* If yes, it's Tier C.

## 3. Progressive depth model

Four layers, matching the Visual Foundations hierarchy ("depth available, never imposed"):

1. **Scroll layer** (everyone): the narrative + animated visuals. Plain language, no jargon.
2. **Expand layer** (curious): inline "How do we know?" / "What's really happening here?" disclosures — provenance lineage, what a cupping session looks like, what Liam actually reads.
3. **Live layer** (skeptics): the Tier-B aggregate signals, visibly timestamped. This is what makes it a *trust* page rather than a claim page.
4. **Method note** (nerds & press): a single linked "Our method" footnote page stating the WCR/SCA alignment with the honest-claim guardrail wording (see §6).

Nothing below layer 1 is required to understand the page. Nothing in any layer crosses into Tier C.

## 4. Page concept — section by section

The spine replaces the current five beats (problem → inputs → engine → loop → CTA) with the data lifecycle. Each section = one lifecycle stage + one visual + one optional depth expander.

### HERO — "This page is a window, not a brochure"
- Working headline territory: *"Every cup you love has a location. Here's how it earns one."* — keeps the loved current line, pivots from asserting the system to showing it.
- Visual: the abstracted live graph, slowly breathing; a small timestamp: "Map last tightened {date}." Sets the live-system tone in the first second.
- One-line promise: we're going to walk you through how sensory reality becomes data, and where AI reads it.

### SECTION 1 — CAPTURE: "Two streams meet at a coordinate"
- Story: a coffee arrives with its field story (origin, process, roast, roaster's own words); then we taste it in structured cupping sessions. Origin is where the data *starts*, not what we match on — this preserves the current page's strongest argument (origin ≠ guarantee) inside the new frame.
- Visual: two streams (field data / measured taste) converging into a single point.
- Depth expander: what a cupping session looks like; "our vocabulary and intensity method are aligned with the WCR Sensory Lexicon and SCA's CVA assessment" (Tier A provenance — most brands can't say where their numbers come from).

### SECTION 2 — STRUCTURE: "How a coffee earns its coordinates"
- Story: raw impressions become an archetype assignment and a position — a *place*, not a poetic tasting note. Introduce the five archetypes here (carried over from current page, they work).
- Visual: a messy cloud of descriptors resolving into a clean placed point within an archetype region.
- Depth expander: why we say a position is provisional at first and hardens with evidence (honest, and sets up Section 5).

### SECTION 3 — CONNECT: "The map is a network, not a shelf"
- Story: every coffee is linked to its neighbors along shared taste dimensions; archetypes are adjacent where real sensory bridges exist. You never fall off the map — every coffee has a "one step brighter / one step deeper" neighbor.
- Visual: the graph lighting up path-by-path (Tier B abstraction: real density, no labels).
- This is the page's most distinctive section — no coffee competitor shows a connected flavor network. Keep the *existence* vivid, the *construction rules* invisible.

### SECTION 4 — CONSUME: "Where AI reads the map" (AI named here)
- Story, two characters:
  - **Liam, the sommelier.** Before Liam speaks with you, he reads the same living map — your position and the coffees near it. Framing: *AI with a map, not AI with a vibe.* Explicitly contrast with generic chatbots: Liam's answers are grounded in structured, measured data.
  - **The builders' AI.** We also use AI to build and tune the system itself — suggesting where a coffee should sit as cupping data accrues, auditing the map's logic. Honest, current, and differentiating; per 2026 consumer research, users increasingly expect brands to disclose where AI operates.
- Visual: one map, many readers — quiz, Liam, recommendations all drawing from the same source ("captured once, read everywhere").
- Depth expander: "What Liam can see (your taste profile, the map) and what he can't (he never invents a coffee that isn't measured)."

### SECTION 5 — REFINE: "A map that tightens itself"
- Story: every rating, reply, and new cupping session is a signal; positions move; confidence grows. The map is deliberately provisional and improving — say so. Admitting provisionality is the credibility move that separates this page from every "our algorithm is perfect" competitor.
- Visual: the feedback cycle + Tier-B live counters ("X signals this month, Y positions refined").
- The customer's role from the current page's loop section survives here, upgraded: your feedback doesn't go into a void — *you can watch the aggregate effect on this page.*

### CTA — unchanged in spirit
- "Find your place on the map." Quiz CTA; microcopy keeps the low-friction tone. Optionally: "…then come back and watch it move."

## 5. Visual & interaction direction

- **Scrollytelling as the delivery mechanism.** The lifecycle is inherently sequential; scroll-driven reveal (one idea per viewport, visuals that transform between sections) is the current standard for "how it works" storytelling (Apple Watch Ultra-style pacing). Ideally one continuous visual — the map — that transforms across all five sections: points appear (capture), settle (structure), link (connect), get read (consume), shift (refine). One protagonist, five states.
- **Calm is a feature.** No urgency, no particle-storm data clichés. Slow, composed motion; the "breathing" graph should feel like a quiet instrument, not a fintech dashboard.
- **Color is data.** Archetype colors carry through every visual; no decorative color.
- **Timestamps everywhere in the live layer.** Nothing says "not a brochure" like a real date.
- Build note: Tier-B aggregates need only a tiny read-only stats endpoint (counts + last-updated) — deliberately no coordinates or labels in the payload, so the API itself can't leak Tier C.

## 6. Copy guardrails (carry into the copy phase)

- **Provenance claim wording (from foundation doc — binding):** "aligned with / built on / calibrated to" the WCR Lexicon and SCA CVA. Never "certified," "accredited," or implying a WCR-accredited lab. Scale described as "coffee-relative calibration, WCR-aligned in method" only in the method note, never as an operational detail.
- **AI wording:** name Liam; say AI plainly; never "magic," never anthropomorphize beyond the sommelier framing. Disclose function ("reads the map," "suggests positions"), not architecture.
- **Keep** from current terminology: flavor space, archetype. **Retire:** "proprietary vector mapping," "multidimensional distance analysis" — assertion-era vocabulary; the new page proves instead of asserts. "Collaborative Flavor Wheel" retired (decision 2026-07-14): replaced by **"Bloom Notes"** (the tasting note you log) feeding **"Taste Memory"** (the accumulating profile) — both rooted in existing brand language; avoids collision with the SCA Flavor Wheel now cited for provenance.
- **Honesty posture:** provisionality is stated, not hidden ("positions harden as evidence accrues"). Guide, don't educate or push: every depth layer is opt-in.

## 7. Trend rationale (July 2026 snapshot)

- Transparency ranked shoppers' #1 brand trait in 2026 — above price for the first time; brands proactively disclosing AI usage and sourcing saw materially higher satisfaction. Trust pages that *demonstrate* rather than claim are the current pattern ("radical transparency," show-don't-tell).
- ~50% of consumers want explicit clarity on when/how a brand uses AI; naming and locating AI (Liam + builders' AI) is now an expectation, not a novelty.
- Coffee specifically: majors (JDE Peet's, Lavazza) are investing in data-driven DTC personalization — "we personalize with data" alone is becoming table stakes. Showing the *pipeline and its provenance* is the defensible layer above it.
- Scrollytelling remains the dominant pattern for narrative "how it works" pages: one idea per scroll step, a single transforming visual.

## 8a. Design inputs & phase-1 packaging constraint (v1.1)

**Archetype→color mapping exists** (`misc/design_documents/Embalagens_v1.pdf`, Camila; base tokens in `frontend/src/styles/theme.css`):

| Bag | Color family |
|---|---|
| Fruity | Pink/coral |
| Floral | Magenta/plum |
| Balanced & Sweet | Mustard/ochre |
| Chocolate & Nutty | Terracotta/burnt orange |
| Spicy & Earthy | Deep red |
| Experimental | Teal |
| Generic | Cream/beige + "Archetype" label area |

These colors are the palette for the page's transforming-map visual ("color is data"). Naming decisions (Dana, 2026-07-14): archetype name is **"Earthy"** ("spicy" adds complication — update bag copy with Camila). **"Experimental" is a badge, not a region**: experimental coffees behave like archetype members — they sit at real coordinates near their archetype kin (floral-leaning, earthy-leaning, etc.), are teal-flagged, and are reachable by hops from archetypes. On the map: an overlay/flag, never a sixth region.

**Phase-1 constraint: only two bags launch — Generic and Experimental** (cost). Archetype identity is carried by a **pasted label in archetype colors** on the generic bag (standard industry practice). Strategic consequences for this page:

- **The journey chain is: label (identity, at a glance) → QR → user profile (recognition: "this was matched for you") → The Axis (explanation & system depth).** QR destination is not final but the working intent is the user profile — which matches Visual Foundations hierarchy exactly: Primary = Identity (profile), Tertiary/Quaternary = Explanation & System Depth (The Axis). The Axis is not the QR landing page; it's the "how did my coffee earn this place?" link *from* the profile.
- Design consequence: The Axis page should be **linkable with archetype context** (e.g., arriving from a Fruity profile highlights the Fruity region of the map) so the explanation continues the recognition rather than restarting generically. One page, entry-aware — not five pages.
- The label in archetype colors makes the color system the *physical↔digital bridge*: same color on the label, in the profile, and on the map. This is "color is data" made literal — worth stating in the visual spec.
- The bag copy already contains the page's best language: *"The Axis is your direction: the profile you naturally return to. The Bloom is what unfolds within it."* and *"Your coffee may change. Your flavor stays close to home."* Carry both into the page copy — packaging, profile, and page must speak identically.
- QR/profile flow (decided): the QR always links to **one URL — the profile page**. The profile page itself resolves state per the customer lifecycle strategy: not logged in → login prompt; logged in without profile/match → quiz prompt; matched → full profile. No special QR landing pages. The Axis stays one hop away as the explanation link from the profile, in every state where a profile exists.

*|FNAME|*,

Finding your taste is a process — one we designed this whole system around. The first bag is a first reading: your palate is the instrument, and every cup after it adds a line to your Taste Memory.

What moves it forward is one small habit: after each bag, tell us what you tasted. What sang, what surprised you, what felt off — one sentence is enough, and every kind counts. “Exactly right” teaches the system as much as “too bitter”; both sharpen the picture, and the next coffee is chosen knowing more about you than the last.

That includes the notes that say something felt off — too bitter, too light, too roasty. Those are exactly what the system is built to use: we fold them into your profile, and your next coffee is chosen with the correction built in. This is the loop working for you — your Taste Memory grows richer with every note, and the coffee that arrives keeps getting closer to the one you'd have chosen yourself.

Getting you right is the whole point of Axis & Bloom, and the people who talk to us are the people we get precisely right.

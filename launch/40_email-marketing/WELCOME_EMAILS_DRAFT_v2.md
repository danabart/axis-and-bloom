## What changed from v1, and why (the sales reframe)

**1. We stopped selling "distance." We're selling the data journey + the AI that reads it.**
v1 leaned on *"distance, not opinion"* — a mechanic that, as you said, interests no one. The Axis page
already made this exact pivot (V2, "Watch the data work"): it sells the *story the data carries* — where
the beans grew, how they were processed and roasted, what the roaster tasted — meeting the data from
*your* quiz, structured into a place on a map an AI can actually read. That's the modern, sellable
version of the same truth. The emails now speak that language: **provenance data + measurement + AI**,
not geometry. "Distance" and "vector" never appear.

**2. Liam leads earlier.** Email 1 now introduces Liam, your AI sommelier, in two lines — and makes the
key point that Liam never improvises: he reads the whole map (your profile, the coffees near it, the
paths between) *before* he says a word. Liam is a reason to believe, and a reason to subscribe.

**3. The "if we're wrong" remedy is now re-match + earned discount — not a free bag.** A free bag invites
free-coffee hunters. Instead: the real fix is a *better match*, and the discount is unlocked only by the
one thing that fixes it — you telling us what missed. Engagement is the price of the perk. It filters for
the customer who wants to be matched, not the one who wants a freebie.

**4. "What arrives" no longer says "the first sip is the real test."** That put pass/fail on us and ignored
that taste is subjective. Reframed: the first bag isn't an exam — it's the first reading. Your palate is the
instrument, not the judge. A cup that lands differently than expected isn't a failed match; it's signal that
sharpens the next one. This email now *sets the expectation* instead of raising the stakes. Moved to day 4.

**5. October 1 offers a bigger AI package, not a launch discount.** No day-one price cut (it trains people to
wait for discounts and attracts the wrong audience). Founding members get an **expanded Liam allowance +
early Bloom Dial access** — depth of experience, not a coupon. It also feeds Liam more signal, faster.

## Email 2 — day 2 · "How we actually found it"
**Preview:** No barista theater. Real data, read by an AI that can't bluff.
**Pillar:** End the guessing / System-led, data-driven

*|FNAME|*,

Most coffee advice starts with someone else's favorite. Ours starts with data — some of it about the
coffee, some of it about you.

Every coffee arrives carrying its own record: origin, process, roast, and the roaster's own tasting.
We keep all of it — but a record can't tell us whether *you'll* love what's in the bag. So we taste.
In structured cupping sessions we measure each coffee across the same sensory dimensions, the same
way, every time, until it earns a position on the map. Origin is where the data starts. It was never
where it ends.

Then the AI goes to work. Your recommendations aren't someone's hunch — they're the coffees the
system reads as closest to where you already are. And it keeps sharpening: AI helps us *build* the
map too, proposing where a coffee's position should move as evidence accumulates, and keeping the
whole thing honest. Humans taste. The AI keeps the bookkeeping straight.

That's the difference between a recommendation with a map behind it and a recommendation with a
vibe. You'll feel it the first time you talk to Liam.

## Email 4 — day 7 · "If we miss, we correct — not comp"
**Preview:** A miss isn't wasted. Here's exactly what we do with it.
**Pillar:** Accountable quality

*|FNAME|*,

A plain promise: if your first match doesn't feel right, we don't just apologize and move on — we
fix the match itself.

Here's how it works, and it asks one thing of you. Tell us what felt off — too bitter, too light, too
roasty. That single piece of feedback is the only thing that can actually correct a coordinate, so it's
the only thing we ask for: no forms, no proving your case. We fold what you tell us into your profile,
re-place you, and choose the next coffee *with* that correction — and that corrected bag comes to you

We'd rather do it this way than hand out a free bag, and the reason is honest: a free bag fixes a
feeling for a day. A better match fixes the thing that went wrong. The customers who take us up on
this are the ones we can genuinely get right — and getting you right is the whole point of Axis & Bloom.

So there's no wrong way to start. There's a first coffee, and a truer second one if we need it.

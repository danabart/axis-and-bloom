## Who is reading, and what these emails are for

**Lifecycle reality (verified in the build):** the `quiz-completed` audience is mixed. Signed-in
members are auto-subscribed after the quiz (lifecycle: quiz taken, no order). But most of the list
will be **guests who left only an email at the results gate** — their archetype is saved on the
subscriber record, but they have **no account, no profile, no household, no Taste Memory**. These
are pre-launch emails, so nothing is purchasable until October 1.

**Therefore the goals of this series, in order:**

1. **Create a profile** — the match survives beyond one browser tab; Taste Memory begins. (E1's job.)
2. **Trust the system** — the data journey + AI story, told like The Axis page. (E2's job.)
3. **Set expectations + invite the household** — how an order works, add the people you share
   coffee with. (E3's job. Nothing about match accuracy here — that lives in E4 only.)
4. **Build the feedback habit** — finding your taste is a process; we want a note after every bag,
   positive or negative, because both sharpen Taste Memory. The email sells the loop's benefit to
   the user (corrections built into the next choice). **No discount is promised in copy** — profile
   tuning is routine, and a reward can't be owed for every adjustment. (E4's job.)
5. **Show up on October 1** — first access + the founding package. (E5's job.)

**Voice rules** (Brand Plate v3.0 / Brand Values v1.0 / The Axis V2, plus Dana 2026-07-23): calm,
warm, precise; no exclamation points; no countdown theatrics; guide, don't push; remember, never
reset. **Positive register only: never name what we're *not* doing, never hand the reader a thought
we don't want them to have.** Banned in copy: "test", "verdict", "exam", "judge", "free bag",
"coupon", "distance", "vector", "algorithm", and "not X, but Y" constructions that lead with X.
Say instead: the map, position, archetype, the data that travels with the beans, first reading,
Liam reads the map, the match gets truer.

**Cadence — spaced so it never reads as pressure:**

**Goal:** Trust in the system; visit /the-axis.

*|FNAME|*,

Most coffee advice starts with someone else's favorite. Ours starts with data — some of it about
the coffee, some of it about you.

Every coffee arrives carrying its own record: origin, process, roast, what the roaster tasted. We
keep all of it — and then we taste for ourselves. In structured cupping sessions we measure each

and flavor is what we match you on.

Then AI goes to work — in two roles, worth telling apart. Behind the scenes, it builds with us:
as evidence accumulates, AI proposes where a coffee's position should move and keeps the
bookkeeping honest, while humans do the tasting. In front of you, it speaks: **Liam, your AI
agent**, reads the finished map — your profile, the coffees closest to it, the paths between
them — and turns it into a conversation. Your recommendations come from that same reading: the
coffees the system finds closest to where you already are.

The whole journey is on display, from a coffee's first record to your recommendation.

## Email 4 — day 21 · "Tell us what you taste"
**Preview:** One sentence after each bag is enough — here's what it does.
**Goal:** Feedback after every bag, positive or negative; the loop's benefit, no promised reward.

*|FNAME|*,

Finding your taste is a process — one we designed this whole system around. The first bag is a
first reading: your palate is the instrument, and every cup after it adds a line to your Taste
Memory.

What moves it forward is one small habit: after each bag, tell us what you tasted. What sang,
what surprised you, what felt off — one sentence is enough, and every kind counts. "Exactly
right" teaches the system as much as "too bitter"; both sharpen the picture, and the next coffee
is chosen knowing more about you than the last.

That includes the notes that say something felt off — too bitter, too light, too roasty. Those
are exactly what the system is built to use: we fold them into your profile, and your next
coffee is chosen with the correction built in. This is the loop working for you — your Taste
Memory grows richer with every note, and the coffee that arrives keeps getting closer to the
one you'd have chosen yourself.

Getting you right is the whole point of Axis & Bloom, and the people who talk to us are the
people we get precisely right.

**[ See your taste profile → your results ]**

— the Axis & Bloom team

### E1 archetype paragraphs (conditional content block per archetype)

Drafted 2026-07-23. Each extends the on-screen reveal description (same sensory vocabulary from
`FlavorQuiz.tsx`) one level deeper, adding what the reader's answers said about them — the
"detailed why" the spec calls for. Implement in Mailchimp as a conditional block on the
ARCHETYPE merge field (or per-`archetype:<name>` tag branches).

**Floral**
Your answers leaned toward the delicate end of flavor — lift over weight, fragrance over force.
In the cup, that means coffees that feel light, elegant, and quietly expressive: jasmine and
soft citrus, a tea-like clarity, sweetness that arrives gently and stays. Yours are the coffees
people call beautiful before they call them anything else.

**Fruity**
Your answers pointed to brightness — flavor that moves. Fruity coffees bring juice and life to
the cup: berries and ripe stone fruit, citrus that sparkles, a tropical sweetness that makes the
morning cup feel awake before you are. Yours is the archetype for palates that want the cup to
do something.

**Balanced & Sweet**
Your answers favored harmony — flavor where everything sits in its place. These coffees are
smooth, round, and quietly generous: caramel and honey, a touch of gentle fruit, a sweetness
that feels effortless. Yours is the cup that's exactly right every morning, before you've asked
anything of it.

**Chocolate & Nutty**
Your answers chose depth you can lean on — the classic register of coffee, done precisely.
Expect cocoa and roasted nuts, a fuller body, and a warm, grounding presence that makes the
first cup feel like solid ground. Yours is the archetype of comfort with a backbone.

**Earthy**
Your answers reached for the deep end — flavor with weight and a long finish. Earthy coffees
open a more grounded sensory world: spice and wood, dark herbs, a trace of smoke, complexity
that unfolds slowly and rewards attention. Yours are the cups that keep offering more.
